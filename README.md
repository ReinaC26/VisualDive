## Overview:
VisualDive is an iOS app designed for those who need to quickly discover, organize, and compare inspirational visual content for their social media posts, presentations, and creative personal projects.

## Features:
- Users can search photos or videos based on keywords, color, name of photographers/creators of videos, width, height, etc. 
- Users can save photos and videos in folders with their customized labels. Users may download the photos and videos. 
- A color palette extractor that allows the extraction of colors based on the images/photos the user uploads. Users may use the extracted color codes to find more similar photos in the search screen. 
- A video workshop screen with a timeline scrubber to preview a specific section of a video, and the user can play both and pause both videos at the same time. They can select up to two different videos for comparison in this screen.
